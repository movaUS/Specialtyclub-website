About

We make beverages —
and brands.

A contract manufacturer that builds and sells its own drinks, then does the same for the brands it partners with.

Started in
Winston-Salem.

Specialty Club Corp is a contract beverage manufacturer in Winston-Salem, North Carolina. We began by formulating and selling our own functional drinks — and grew into a full-service co-packer that does the same for other brands.

Formulation, filling, canning, packaging, and design all happen under one roof. Fewer vendors, tighter timelines, and one team accountable from recipe to pallet.

We work best with small and mid-size brands who need a partner flexible enough to run a trial and capable enough to scale it — especially in functional and cannabinoid categories, where compliance and consistency matter most.
