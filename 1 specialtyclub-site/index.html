Specialty Club — 12 fl oz

It starts as a formula.

Every run begins in the lab, not on the line. We build the flavor system, adjust for shelf stability, and lock the specification before a single can is filled.

Dose is a manufacturing problem.

Nano-emulsion carries the cannabinoid into suspension so it stays evenly distributed through the run, under our GMP quality program.

The panel is part of the product.

Ingredient, nutrition, allergen, regulatory, and packaging requirements are reviewed alongside your artwork before production. We help ensure that the selected packaging format, labeling, and product specifications align with your formulation and manufacturing needs.

And it leaves as yours.

Formulation, fill, packaging and compliance in a single line. Your brand on the can, and our name nowhere on it.
