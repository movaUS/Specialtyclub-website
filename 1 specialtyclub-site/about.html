About

We make beverages —
and brands.

A contract manufacturer that builds and sells its own drinks, then does the same for the brands it partners with.

Virginia Beach
to North Carolina.

Specialty Club Corp is a North Carolina–based contract beverage manufacturer. We started in Virginia Beach in 2014, developing and selling our own functional drinks, and expanded into Winston-Salem as we grew into a full-service partner that builds beverages for other brands too.

We're a single source from concept to finished product — development, ingredient sourcing, formulation, carbonation, high-speed canning, packaging, and fulfillment, all under one roof. Fewer vendors, tighter timelines, one team accountable from recipe to pallet.

We produce carbonated and non-carbonated ready-to-drink beverages across categories — functional and wellness drinks, teas, energy, probiotic sodas, cannabinoid, and formulations others haven't tried yet. From emerging labels launching a first flavor to established brands scaling a proven one, we're built to run the volume in front of us — with the quality assurance and batch traceability retail demands.
